Keyboard Shortcuts Reference

Global & in-app hotkeys for seamless, invisible interview navigation.

Ctrl + Shift + S
Snip Screen & Solve Instantly freeze screen and draw a box over any coding problem, MCQ, or diagram to get immediate AI solution.
Ctrl + Shift + C
Clear Answers / Panic Instantly wipe all solutions from the screen and reset session conversation memory.
Ctrl + [
Increase Glass Transparency Decreases opacity by 10% making the overlay window stealthier and harder to detect.
Ctrl + ]
Increase Glass Opacity Increases opacity by 10% to make answer text more solid and readable against bright backgrounds.
Ctrl + M
Click-Through (Pass-Through) Mode Makes the window transparent to mouse clicks so you can click apps underneath. Auto-resets in 30s or press Ctrl+M.
Spacebar (Hold)
Push-to-Talk Candidate Mic Hold Spacebar while in Copilot view to speak your question directly to AI, and release to send instantly.
Enter
Submit Manual Question Type or paste any interview question in the bottom dock and press Enter for immediate answer.

How to Use This Software (Step-by-Step)

1
Setup Your Profile & Resume (Mandatory)

Go to the Profile tab. Enter your full name, valid email address, 10-digit mobile number, and upload your PDF resume. The AI will personalize every interview answer around your real experience and projects.

2
Start the Live Copilot

On the Home tab, click the Start Copilot button. The app switches into an ultra-sleek, semi-transparent workspace ready for your interview meeting.

3
Interviewer Audio Listening (Loopback)

By default, the Loopback button is active. It captures the interviewer's voice directly from your system speakers (Teams, Zoom, Google Meet). Smart Voice Activity Detection (VAD) automatically answers when the interviewer stops talking.

4
Screen Capture & Problem Solving

When given a coding challenge, system design problem, or MCQ on screen, press Ctrl+Shift+S or click Snip. Drag a box over the problem. The AI reads it via OCR and provides code, complexity analysis, and approach.

5
Phone Companion / Mobile Stealth

Scan the QR code on the Home screen with your mobile phone camera. Live answers will stream to your phone screen in real-time. You can minimize the desktop window using Phone Stealth for 100% undetected assistance.

6
Quick Notes & Talking Points

Use the Notes tab to save your introduction, behavioral stories, and system design formulas. Click the Notes button inside live Copilot to view your cheat sheet without leaving the meeting.

7
Credits & Session History

Visit the Credit & History tab to view your remaining points and review past interview sessions. Redeem 16-character voucher codes anytime to recharge credits.
